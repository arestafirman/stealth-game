MOTHLIGHT — a lamplight stealth. You are a moth slipping through three lamplit rooms after closing: a museum gallery, a vault, and a rooftop. Guards patrol with bright yellow vision cones; dark floor and bushes hide you, crates block their sight. Pocket the golden moths, reach the green exit unseen, and earn the GHOST rating if no guard ever spots you.

- 3 hand-built levels (Museum, Vault, Rooftop) with patrol routes you can learn
- Detection that gives you a chance: calm → suspicious → chase → search, with the meter draining once you break sight
- Sneak (Shift) to move slow and quiet; footsteps and a guard's pulse are synthesized live with WebAudio
- Top-down 2D canvas, vanilla JavaScript, no libraries, fully offline — no network requests

## Run it

`node serve.ts` lalu buka http://localhost:8080

WASD / arrows to move, Shift to sneak, Enter to start, R to restart.
